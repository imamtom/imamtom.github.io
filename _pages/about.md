Li Wenjie received the Ph.D. degree from Xidian University in 2025. He is currently a Lecturer with the School of Computer and Cyber Security, Hebei Normal University. His research interests include federated (un)learning and trustworthy artificial intelligence. From 2023 to 2024, he was a visiting student at the College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS), Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. He has published some papers with <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=wATzPDYAAAAJ'><img src="https://img.shields.io/endpoint?logo=Google%20Scholar&url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.jsdelivr.net%2Fgh%2Fimamtom%2Fimamtom.github.io%40google-scholar-stats%2Fgs_data_shieldsio.json&labelColor=f6f6f6&color=9cf&style=flat&label=citations"></a>.

# 🎓 Prospective Students

I am actively looking for motivated **M.Sc. students** to join my group at the [School of Computer and Cyber Security, Hebei Normal University](https://it.hebtu.edu.cn/). If you have a strong interest in **cyber security** research, please get in touch.

- Solid programming skills in **Python** with hands-on experience building and training models in **PyTorch**
- Familiarity with deep learning models (e.g., CNNs, Transformers, federated learning frameworks)
- Genuine research interest in **cyber security**, privacy, or trustworthy AI
- Self-driven, comfortable reading and discussing recent papers in English, and willing to do rigorous empirical work

Please email me at [liwenjie@hebtu.edu.cn](mailto:liwenjie@hebtu.edu.cn) with your CV / résumé and a short paragraph describing your research interests and any prior projects
